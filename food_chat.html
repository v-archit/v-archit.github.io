Food Delivery AI

Goal Oriented Action Planning Simulation

Role: Lead Programmer (Artificial Intelligence in Games)
Engine: Unity (C#)
Platform: Windows

Overview

Food Delivery AI is a simulation that demonstrates the use of Goal Oriented Action Planning (GOAP) for coordinating multiple autonomous agents to achieve a complex objective.
The system dynamically evaluates world states and determines the optimal sequence of actions needed to deliver food orders from preparation to final delivery.

Architecture

                            Order System
                               |-- GOAP Planner
                               |     |-- Goal Evaluation
                               |     |-- Action Selection
                               |
                               |-- Agent Controllers
                               |     |-- Bike Agent (Ingredient Delivery)
                               |     |-- Chef Agent (Food Preparation)
                               |     |-- Car Agent (Final Delivery)

The system coordinates multiple agents to complete the food delivery pipeline. The planner determines the optimal sequence of actions required to satisfy incoming orders.

Key Systems

GOAP Planning System

The AI planner evaluates available actions and dynamically generates the most efficient sequence of steps to achieve the goal of delivering food.

Multi-Agent Task Coordination

Three specialized agents collaborate to complete the delivery process: a bike agent transports ingredients, a chef prepares the food, and a car delivers the final order.

Challenges & Solutions

Agent Coordination

Synchronizing multiple agents required dynamic goal evaluation and prioritized action planning to avoid conflicts and delays.

Efficient Planning

Action search complexity was reduced by structuring goals and actions into a hierarchical planning system.

Performance & Optimization

  • Used coroutine execution for efficient agent behaviour
